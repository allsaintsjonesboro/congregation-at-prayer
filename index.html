Catechism — The Ten Commandments
⌄

The Third Commandment

Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy. Preschool +

What does this mean? 2nd Grade +

We should fear and love God so that we do not despise preaching and His Word, but hold it sacred and gladly hear and learn it.

The Fourth Commandment

Honor your father and your mother. Preschool +

What does this mean? 2nd Grade +

We should fear and love God so that we do not despise or anger our parents and other authorities, but honor them, serve and obey them, love and cherish them.

Catechesis Notes for the Week

How are we to remember the Sabbath day? By spending it in the holy things of God. God gave the Sabbath to mankind as a gift of needed rest. And while Christ Jesus has become our Sabbath rest through His fulfillment of the Law in His death and resurrection, we receive Christ's rest as we are gathered around the holy means of Word and Sacrament. This is why when we skip the Divine Service, we sin not just against the Third Commandment but also against the First, for we are putting earthly things ahead of holy things. And then the things of this world, or perhaps we ourselves, are becoming our god. God has arranged to have His Word preached to us each week that our sins might be forgiven anew and that our faith might be strengthened for life as His people living in a sin-filled world. Let us not despise preaching and His Word, but hold it sacred and gladly hear and learn it.

In order that our life might go well in this world, God has also set up particular offices of authority and order. The first and most important offices in all of creation are those of father and mother. God has given parents a most lofty responsibility and divine authority in raising up children in the way they should go. They are to care for and nurture them, provide for and protect them. Most of all, they are to pass along the faith, to catechize them in the Gospel of Christ, that they may know and cling to God their Father and Christ their Savior. For this reason, parents are to be loved and cherished, honored and obeyed by their children, that this responsibility might be for them a joy and not a burden. In addition, as extensions of the offices of father and mother, God has established other offices of authority in the world. These include pastors, teachers, just rulers, and the like. These, too, serve God and represent Him in their particular offices. As they carry out their responsibilities faithfully, they remind us that God is both just and merciful, firm and loving. God grant those in positions of authority the desire, strength, and patience to do so.

Prayer for Ourselves and Others
⌄
Sunday
Pray for the joy of the resurrection among us; for the fruit of faith nourished by the Word and Sacraments.
Monday
Pray for faith to live in the promises of Holy Baptism; for one's calling and daily work; for the unemployed; for the salvation and well-being of our neighbors; for schools, colleges, and seminaries; for good government and for peace.
Tuesday
Pray for deliverance against temptation and evil; for the addicted and despairing, the tortured and oppressed; for those struggling with besetting sins.
Wednesday
Pray for marriage and family, that husbands and wives, parents and children live in ordered harmony according to the Word of God; for parents who must raise children alone; for our communities and neighborhoods.
Thursday
Pray for the Church and her pastors; for teachers, deacons, deaconesses, and other church workers; for missionaries and for all who serve the Church; for fruitful and salutary use of the blessed sacrament of Christ's body and blood.
Friday
Pray for the preaching of the holy cross of our Lord Jesus Christ and for the spread of His knowledge throughout the whole world; for the persecuted and oppressed; for the sick and dying.
Saturday
Pray for faithfulness to the end; for the renewal of those who are withering in the faith or have fallen away; for receptive hearts and minds to God's Word on the Lord's Day; for pastors and people as they prepare to administer and receive Christ's holy gifts.
