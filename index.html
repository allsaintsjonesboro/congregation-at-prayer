Catechesis Notes — A New Season of Catechesis and Prayer

As we begin a new catechetical year this week, we go to the beginning of the Small Catechism with our nine month walk through the Six Chief Parts and the Table of Duties. Each school year we will "pray through the Catechism," meditating each week upon the truths of God's Word according to the pattern laid out in the Catechism.

The Ten Commandments preach repentance. The message of God's Law is necessary in order that we might receive the Gospel with repentant hearts. The Creed preaches that Gospel to us by which we are saved from our sin and through which we are given the strength to make a beginning at keeping the Ten Commandments. The Creed is "the faith that saves and sanctifies us" as it proclaims all that our God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, has done for us for our life and salvation.

Out of this faith in Christ the fruits of faith flow in love and service to the neighbor. Out of this faith in Christ we learn to call God "Our Father" in the Lord's Prayer, calling upon Him for every need that He has promised to meet in the Creed.

The next three Chief Parts speak of the Word and Sacraments of Christ by which the faith of the Creed is imparted to us and by which that faith is continually nurtured, nourished, and sustained. The Sacrament of Holy Baptism makes us Christians. By Holy Baptism we are united to Christ's death and resurrection, clothed with Christ's righteousness, given the gift of the Holy Spirit, and declared to be the beloved Children of God. Under Confession and the Office of the Keys we learn that it is the ongoing preaching of the Gospel of Jesus' forgiveness in sermons, catechesis, and absolution that our faith is continually refreshed in the struggle against sin and doubt. In the Sacrament of the Altar we are given Jesus' very body and blood as the highest expression of God's love for us in the Gospel that we might live continually in His forgiveness and learn to love God and our neighbor, even as Christ loves us freely.

The Sacrament of the Altar leads us into the Table of Duties where our faith in Christ takes shape in our vocation to love our pastors, spouses, children, employers, and coworkers, as Jesus has loved us. St. Paul says, "Hold fast the pattern of sound words which you have heard from me, in faith and love which are in Christ Jesus" (2 Tim. 1:13). The Small Catechism gives us "the pattern of sound words" by which we learn to know ourselves rightly and to know our God and Savior Jesus Christ. For this reason, we continually meditate upon the truths of the Catechism that we might be grounded in the faith of the Holy Scriptures and the Gospel of Jesus Christ all the days of our lives.

— Rev. Peter Bender

Prayer for Ourselves and Others
⌄
Sunday
Pray for the joy of the resurrection among us; for the fruit of faith nourished by the Word and Sacraments.
Monday
Pray for faith to live in the promises of Holy Baptism; for one's calling and daily work; for the unemployed; for the salvation and well-being of our neighbors; for schools, colleges, and seminaries; for good government and for peace.
Tuesday
Pray for deliverance against temptation and evil; for the addicted and despairing, the tortured and oppressed; for those struggling with besetting sins.
Wednesday
Pray for marriage and family, that husbands and wives, parents and children live in ordered harmony according to the Word of God; for parents who must raise children alone; for our communities and neighborhoods.
Thursday
Pray for the Church and her pastors; for teachers, deacons, deaconesses, and other church workers; for missionaries and for all who serve the Church; for fruitful and salutary use of the blessed sacrament of Christ's body and blood.
Friday
Pray for the preaching of the holy cross of our Lord Jesus Christ and for the spread of His knowledge throughout the whole world; for the persecuted and oppressed; for the sick and dying.
Saturday
Pray for faithfulness to the end; for the renewal of those who are withering in the faith or have fallen away; for receptive hearts and minds to God's Word on the Lord's Day; for pastors and people as they prepare to administer and receive Christ's holy gifts.
