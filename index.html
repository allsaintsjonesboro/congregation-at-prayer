7

Catechism — The Lord's Prayer
⌄

Second Petition

Thy kingdom come. Preschool +

What does this mean? 1st Grade +

The kingdom of God certainly comes by itself without our prayer, but we pray in this petition that it may come to us also.

How does God's kingdom come? 2nd Grade +

God's kingdom comes when our heavenly Father gives us His Holy Spirit, so that by His grace we believe His holy Word and lead godly lives here in time and there in eternity.

Third Petition

Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Preschool +

What does this mean? 1st Grade +

The good and gracious will of God is done even without our prayer, but we pray in this petition that it may be done among us also.

How is God's will done? 3rd Grade +

God's will is done when He breaks and hinders every evil plan and purpose of the devil, the world, and our sinful nature, which do not want us to hallow God's name or let His kingdom come; and when He strengthens and keeps us firm in His Word and faith until we die. This is His good and gracious will.

Catechesis Notes for the Week

Second Petition: God's kingdom comes by His Word. When the Word is preached and we, through the work of the Holy Spirit, believe it, His kingdom comes to us. For it is through faith, through that trust relationship in Christ and His work of salvation, that we become God's children. As children we are heirs of His kingdom and the joy and peace that come with it. Notice that we can't become part of God's kingdom of our own volition. It's impossible. We can't choose to be a part of the royal family. Rather, God brings us in; He makes us part of His kingdom by giving us His Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit first works faith in us through the Word, and then as we have been brought to faith, the Holy Spirit works holiness of life in us. Because God's kingdom has come to us, we are now set free from sin, enabled to withstand temptation, and to live a life pleasing to God.

9
Intercede
Prayer for Ourselves and Others
⌄
Sunday
Pray for the joy of the resurrection among us; for the fruit of faith nourished by the Word and Sacraments.
Monday
Pray for faith to live in the promises of Holy Baptism; for one's calling and daily work; for the unemployed; for the salvation and well-being of our neighbors; for schools, colleges, and seminaries; for good government and for peace.
Tuesday
Pray for deliverance against temptation and evil; for the addicted and despairing, the tortured and oppressed; for those struggling with besetting sins.
Wednesday
Pray for marriage and family, that husbands and wives, parents and children live in ordered harmony according to the Word of God; for parents who must raise children alone; for our communities and neighborhoods.
Thursday
Pray for the Church and her pastors; for teachers, deacons, deaconesses, and other church workers; for missionaries and for all who serve the Church; for fruitful and salutary use of the blessed sacrament of Christ's body and blood.
Friday
Pray for the preaching of the holy cross of our Lord Jesus Christ and for the spread of His knowledge throughout the whole world; for the persecuted and oppressed; for the sick and dying.
Saturday
Pray for faithfulness to the end; for the renewal of those who are withering in the faith or have fallen away; for receptive hearts and minds to God's Word on the Lord's Day; for pastors and people as they prepare to administer and receive Christ's holy gifts.
